# B.1. Data Source of IR-TabQA

[Appendix contents](../../README.md) · [Appendix B contents](README.md)

The tables of bench are collected from publicly available internet resources and license-cleared industrial sources. The internet sources include municipal open data platforms, the official website of the National Bureau of Statistics, industry association portals, and open-source tabular datasets, with data sources shown in Table [7](#table-7).
The industrial sources include commercially acquired tables and industrial reports from professional data service providers, as well as anonymized tables from practical industrial applications. We strictly verify redistribution permissions with all data providers prior to inclusion, and only incorporate non-public data that explicitly permits unrestricted academic redistribution. As a result, every table within the released benchmark is fully license-cleared and can be publicly distributed together with its annotations without legal restrictions. All purchased data are acquired under legitimate open licenses, and proprietary industrial data are fully anonymized. Without constraints from commercial agreements, the dataset can be publicly released in its entirety.
